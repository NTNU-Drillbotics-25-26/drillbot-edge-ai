Manual mode allows direct joystick control of rotation and hoisting.

# Rotation (Azimuth Control)

**To rotate the drillstring:**
- Move the right joystick **horizontally**
- **Left** = Counter-clockwise rotation
- **Right** = Clockwise rotation
- No button press needed

**Use for:**
- Adjusting toolface orientation
- Manual azimuth control during slide drilling

# Hoisting

**To hoist or lower:**
- Hold the **red back button** on the right joystick
- Move stick **vertically** while holding the button
- **Pull back** = Hoist (lift the bit)
- **Push forward** = Lower (apply weight to bit)

**Safety note:** The back button acts as a dead-man switch - releasing it stops hoisting/lowering motion.

**Speed:** Movement speed is proportional to stick deflection.
