# Flow Troubleshooting

Flow is essential for cooling the bit and removing cuttings. Loss of flow can cause overheating and stuck bit.

**If there is no flow or flow has dropped, check:**
1. Water supply valve is open
2. Flow choke underneath the table is opened
3. Hoses are not kinked or blocked
4. Water reservoir has adequate level

**To adjust flow rate:**
- Use the flow choke valve located underneath the drill table
- Turn clockwise to reduce flow, counter-clockwise to increase

**Symptoms of inadequate flow:**
- Rising bit temperature
- Poor cuttings removal
- Increased torque
- Potential stuck bit

**Recommended action:** Establish flow before starting rotation to prevent dry drilling.
