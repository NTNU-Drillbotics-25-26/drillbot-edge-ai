# Pressure Troubleshooting

Pressure indicates the state of the fluid circulation system. Abnormal pressure readings require investigation.

**No pressure or low pressure:**
- Indicates no flow or a leak in the system
- Check flow troubleshooting steps first
- Inspect hose connections for leaks

**Troubleshooting low pressure:**
1. Verify water supply is flowing
2. Check for leaks at connections
3. Ensure flow choke is open
4. Check pump operation (if applicable)

**High or increasing pressure:**
- Indicates blockage or restriction in the flow path
- May indicate pack-off downhole

**Troubleshooting high pressure:**
1. Check for kinked hoses
2. Verify annulus is clear of cuttings
3. Reduce WOB and circulate to clear cuttings
4. Inspect bit nozzles for blockage
