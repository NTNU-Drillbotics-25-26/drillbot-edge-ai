# Torque Troubleshooting

Torque spikes indicate resistance to rotation, often caused by formation conditions or mechanical issues.

**Common causes of high torque:**
- Stuck bit (most common)
- Hard formation or formation change
- Pack-off from cuttings buildup
- Rod windup from azimuth cable
- Inadequate drilling fluid flow

**Troubleshooting steps:**
1. Check if stuck bit alarm is also active
2. Reduce WOB to relieve pressure on the bit
3. If stuck, hoist slightly and re-enter
4. Verify flow rate is adequate for cuttings removal
5. Check for rod windup (azimuth cable tangled)

**In autonomous mode:** The system handles torque spikes automatically by hoisting and re-entering.

**In manual mode:** Operator must monitor torque and take corrective action manually.
