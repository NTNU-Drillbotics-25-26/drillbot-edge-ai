A safety plan is required for competition and is graded by judges.

**Requirement:** The safety plan must consider foreseeable hazards and how they are mitigated.

**Not sufficient:** Personal protective equipment alone does not meet requirements.

**Hazards to address:**
- Heavy rock handling
- Rotating machinery
- Electrical shock
- Fluid leaks
- Team communication during work and testing

**Judging:** Teams are graded on the quality of their safety case.

**Good safety plan includes:**
1. Hazard identification
2. Risk assessment
3. Mitigation measures
4. Emergency procedures
5. Communication protocols
