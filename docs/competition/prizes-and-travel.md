Winning teams may receive a travel grant to attend the next SPE or IADC drilling conference, subject to approval and available funds.

The travel support is limited to up to 5 students and 1 supervisor.

The winning team may be asked to submit a paper for conference presentation.

Drillbotics also states that students may receive recognition through other SPE events if only one of the groups is selected by the main conference program committee.
