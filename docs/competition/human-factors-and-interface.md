The design should show who the operators are, which functions are automated, which are manual, and how the operator remains in the loop.

The workflow of the rig, the control and feedback needs of the operator, and the concept of the interface should be described.

A simple wireframe is acceptable if it demonstrates good human factors practice.

The guidelines emphasize appropriate automation, operator awareness, alarm handling, and interface usability.
