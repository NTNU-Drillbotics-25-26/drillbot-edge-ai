Teams must provide a definitive directional survey in TXT, LAS, or CSV format.

The header must include team or school name, survey date, well center coordinates, TVD reference, grid convergence, magnetic model or references, total azimuth correction, total magnetic field reference, and the trajectory error model.

Each survey station must include measured depth, inclination, azimuth referenced to block north, true vertical depth, northing, easting, and dogleg severity.
