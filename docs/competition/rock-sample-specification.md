For the final demonstration, the sample should be a homogeneous sandstone block of about 12 inches by 24 inches by 24 inches, or about 30 by 60 by 60 centimeters.

Teams must acquire or produce their own rock samples for development and testing.

Drilling of DSATS-provided samples before the Phase II test is not allowed, except for the pilot hole drilled at the test location.
