By entering the competition, teams agree to the published rules, including later revisions and FAQs.

Winning teams and finalists must agree to publication of names, photographs, and final paper.

Personal contact details are not intended for publication, but submitted design material is reviewed by the committee.

Previous years’ reports, photos, and similar material may be made public to support open learning across teams.
