Downhole sensors are mandatory.

Their data must be integrated into the control algorithm.

Closed-loop control based on downhole data is mandatory.

Not integrating downhole sensor data into the control algorithm is treated as a failing grade.

To reduce risk during testing, the guidelines recommend redundant or immediately replaceable downhole sensor components.
