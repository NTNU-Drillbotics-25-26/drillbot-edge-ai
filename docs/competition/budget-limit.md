Teams advancing to Phase II were expected to limit physical or virtual rig and materials costs to 10,000 US dollars per year, or the currency equivalent.

Transport of the rig to local test sites is excluded.

Student and supervisor travel is excluded.

In-kind donations from DSATS or other sources do not count toward the cost limit.

Teams spending more than the limit may be penalized.
