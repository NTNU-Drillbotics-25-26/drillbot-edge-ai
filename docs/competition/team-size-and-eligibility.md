The core team must have at least 3 students and no more than 5.

Additional contributors are allowed, but the core team size limit still applies.

At least one core team member should be a petroleum engineering candidate with enough coursework to understand drilling physics and mitigation practices.

Up to two universities may collaborate as one team.

A university may enter more than one team and may enter one or both competition groups.
