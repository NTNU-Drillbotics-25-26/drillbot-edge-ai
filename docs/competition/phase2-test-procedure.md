Before the final test, teams may manually pre-drill a vertical pilot hole using the competition rig.

The pilot hole must not be more than 1 inch deep from the rock top face.

During the competition run, drilling continues from the pilot hole to the kickoff point, which may be any depth greater than 4 inches below the rock surface.

The targets are provided on the day of the test.

Once drilling begins, the run continues until the bit exits the rock or 3 hours have elapsed, whichever happens first.
