Students may design their own tool joints, bit sub, and related drillstring components, but the design concept must be documented in the Phase I report.

Stabilizers are permitted.

Students must add sensors to the drillstring, but they are not allowed to instrument the rock sample.

Along-string sensors and BHA sensors must be at least 10 percent smaller in diameter than the stabilizers and bit.

DSATS does not provide tubing. Teams must source their own.
