The Phase I design report should include student biographies, a safety plan, rig sketches or drawings, design notes and calculations, control architecture, proposed user interface, cost estimate and funding plan, key model and control features, and planned data handling.

The report should explain how downhole data is used for steering and how the system is intended to hit the targets.

The report should also discuss whether the major design features are scalable to field drilling and what would be needed for implementation.

A short design summary video of up to 5 minutes was optional.
