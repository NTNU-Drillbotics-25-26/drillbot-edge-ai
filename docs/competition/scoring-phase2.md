Phase II scoring evaluates creative ability, engineering skills, construction quality, cost control, drilling performance, wellbore quality, and data handling.

**Scoring categories:**

| Category | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Trajectory / Test / Target Accuracy | 25 |
| Drilling Efficiency (Time to TD / ROP) | 25 |
| Well-Control & Dysfunction Handling | 15 |
| Safety & Compliance | 10 |
| Human Factors & UI Clarity | 10 |
| Automation Quality & Innovation | 15 |
| **Subtotal** | **100** |
| Edge-AI Bonus (Optional) | +10 max |
| **Maximum Total** | **110** |

**Key focus areas:**
- Target accuracy and trajectory control (25 points)
- Drilling speed and efficiency (25 points)
- Automation and innovation (15 points)
