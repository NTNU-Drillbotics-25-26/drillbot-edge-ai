The Phase I report should include engineering calculations, assumptions, variable definitions, references, and a summary table of key limits such as buckling, burst, and torque.

It should also include expected electrical power consumption by subsystem.

Teams must provide the operational dimensions of the rig.

Teams must calculate chargeable weight for shipping, which is the larger of actual gross weight and dimensional weight.

Using centimeters, dimensional weight in kilograms is calculated as length times width times height divided by 6000.
