Drilling mode and survey mode switching must be automated.

Steering requirements such as toolface direction and slide length must be calculated autonomously.

The directional surveying process must be fully autonomous.

Survey qualification must be done autonomously, although a human may perform a secondary check or override.

Dogleg severity required to hit the targets, and distance and direction to plan, must be calculated autonomously at each survey station and shown on the rig floor display.
