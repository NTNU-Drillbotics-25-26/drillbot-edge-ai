Teams must be prepared to drill a trajectory that is only revealed immediately before the Phase II test.

The starting plan to hit the targets will not require more than 30 degrees inclination from vertical, 15 degrees total azimuth change, or 10 inches total displacement from the well center.

These are maximum limits, not guaranteed values.

Teams should be ready for smaller build rates as well.
