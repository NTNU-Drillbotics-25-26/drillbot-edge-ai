Basic safety rules apply whenever working with or near the rig.

**Required PPE:**
- Safety glasses: Mandatory in the lab area at all times
- Task-specific PPE for lifting, cement handling, and cuttings management

**Stop work authority:** Any person may stop the work if they observe an unsafe condition. This is a fundamental safety principle.

**Before operating:**
1. Ensure protective cabinet is closed
2. Verify E-stop is accessible
3. Check that all personnel are clear of the rig
4. Confirm PPE is worn

**During operation:**
- Monitor alarms and system status
- Keep hands clear of rotating components
- Follow local lab signage for additional requirements

**Additional PPE may be required for:**
- Rock sample handling (gloves)
- Cement mixing (dust mask, gloves)
- Heavy lifting (steel-toed boots)
