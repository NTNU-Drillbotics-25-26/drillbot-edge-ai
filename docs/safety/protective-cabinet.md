The rig is enclosed by an acrylic protective cabinet to shield the operator from hazards during drilling.

**Purpose:**
- Protects from rotating parts
- Contains possible projectiles (rock chips, broken bits)
- Prevents contact with moving components

**Cabinet sections:**
- **Upper section:** Covers rotating components above the drill floor
- **Lower section:** Encloses the rock sample and drill string

**Operating rules:**
1. Both sections must be closed during drilling operations
2. Only open for maintenance or sample handling
3. Never operate the rig with panels open

**Before opening the cabinet:**
1. Stop all drilling operations
2. Disable drives
3. Wait for all rotation to stop completely
