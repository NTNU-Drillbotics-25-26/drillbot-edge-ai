The rig uses a slide drilling concept for independent bit rotation and directional control.

**How slide drilling works:**
- The **top drive** rotates the drill bit through the internal rod
- The **drill pipe** (outer tube) is rotated by the azimuth system only when direction changes are needed
- Bit rotation and pipe orientation are independent

**Components involved:**
- **Top drive motor:** Rotates the internal rod and bit
- **Azimuth motor:** Rotates the drill pipe for directional control
- **Universal joint:** Allows bit to rotate while pipe is stationary

**Why slide drilling:**
This concept allows the bit to drill continuously while the toolface (bent sub orientation) can be adjusted independently. This is essential for directional drilling where the drilling direction needs to be controlled.

**During directional drilling:** The bit rotates continuously while the azimuth system positions the bent sub to steer the wellbore.
