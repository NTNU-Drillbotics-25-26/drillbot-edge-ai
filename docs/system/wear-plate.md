The wear plate is a consumable component that protects critical BHA parts from damage.

**Material:** PEEK (Polyether Ether Ketone) - a durable engineering plastic

**Location:** Between the bit sub and lower stabilizer in the BHA

**Purpose:**
- Absorbs wear from rotation
- Protects against abrasive cuttings
- Prevents damage to steel components
- Extends life of expensive parts

**Why PEEK:**
- High wear resistance
- Good chemical resistance
- Lower cost than steel replacements
- Easy to replace

**Replacement:**
- The wear plate is a consumable part
- Must be replaced periodically based on wear
- Inspect during maintenance or between runs
- Replace when significant wear grooves or thinning is visible

**Failure to replace:** Worn plates can lead to damage to the bit sub or stabilizer, which are more expensive to repair.
